Devices vision project01
Devices in context
What we build, and why it matters

What we do: the Devices team builds and manages all of Adyen's in-person payment devices, both terminals and mobile (SoftPOS).

Why it matters: in-person payments are €311B, ~23% of Adyen's volume, growing ~35% a year.

The gap: IPP NPS is 57.7 vs 66.2 overall (8.5 points lower) and escalations rose to 3.5%. The experience is the opportunity.

The device lifecycle
  • 01Order & fulfil: order the right hardware, ship, replace and return.
  • 02Onboard & board: bring terminals live and assign them to stores.
  • 03Configure: set payment features, POS limits and branding, via reusable profiles.
  • 04Operate, monitor & update: watch fleet health, fix counter issues, roll out firmware/SDK updates.
  • 05Reassign & retire: move terminals across stores, repair and return.
Dependencies with Channels

Devices is the backend controlling the whole terminal and SoftPOS layer, with a hardware abstraction layer (middleware) that keeps the fleet vendor-independent; Channels owns the unified, device-independent application that runs on top. At runtime Devices supplies device health and diagnostics; Channels renders them on-device, including error messages and self-serve actions.

Devices vision project02
The North Star
from today's fragmented journeys to jobs-based surfaces

Today: a dozen CA and back-office pages for the same few actions, fragmented, duplicated and hard to support.

Future: Devices built around jobs, not the tools we've accumulated, with intelligence embedded.

Why we need it: to align on one direction, so each near-term build adds up to a coherent whole.

  • 01Fleet health, diagnostics & updates: see the health of every terminal, let store staff fix counter issues before they stop a sale, and roll out firmware/SDK updates safely across the fleet.Fleet health is the first slice we build here.Device intelligence
  • 02Lifecycle, onboarding & reassignment: order, ship, replace and return terminals (with stock, repairs and warranties), and onboard or reassign them across stores by business need, without part-mapping or assignment errors.Devices & locations
  • 03Configuration & profiles: build reusable configuration profiles (Company → Merchant → Store → Device), set payment features and POS limits, and brand the terminal screen.Device studio
AI is the thread across all three surfaces, enabling the entire job to be done, not just showing data.
Ask anything
Query fleet, payments and device health in plain language. No dashboards to assemble.
Act by prompt
Onboard, reassign, configure and run bulk fleet actions by describing the outcome.
Next best action
AI surfaces what to do next and why, turning insight into decisions and self-serve.
Devices vision project03
First building block: Fleet health
Inside Device intelligence, where the pain is sharpest and the data already exists. WIP, 2027.
Why here, and what we're testing
  • Hardware fails at the counter. #1 detractor, ~9,100 tickets.
  • No actionable fleet reporting. Reporting 5.74/7, 2nd-lowest.
  • Troubleshooting is reactive. Most tickets from live-integration merchants.

"Why isn't this terminal working?" · "Which of my stores need attention right now?" · "I can't even see which terminals are locked, or why." Merchant

The design: four building blocks
  • 01Fleet health overview: an at-a-glance view across the fleet.
  • 02What to act on: the device list, down to individual logs.
  • 03Fleet trends: the metrics to improve, over time.
  • 04Stores to watch: which stores need attention, into store status.

Fleet-wide → Store → Single device.
