Devices vision project05
First step: Fleet health
Inside Device intelligence. Where the pain is sharpest and the data already exists. WIP, 2027.
Why here: the specific pain
  • Hardware fails at the counter. #1 detractor, ~9,100 tickets.
  • No actionable fleet reporting. Reporting 5.74/7, 2nd-lowest.
  • Troubleshooting is reactive. 70% of tickets from live merchants.
Why now: the data already exists
  • Live telemetry: connectivity, websocket health, reboots.
  • Device health: battery, firmware / SDK, lifespan.
  • Logs and a per-device timeline, available today.

Sources: POS logs dashboard · Ops / partner interviews · fleet-intelligence research.

Hypotheses we're testing
  • 1 · Self-serve troubleshootingAdvanced logs so that merchants self-serve and Adyen gets fewer tickets.
  • 2 · Control through metricsFleet health metrics so that merchants act and feel in control.
